              THE IDEA

              BIG ENERGY.
              SMALL SCOPE.

              Academic Arena starts with one thing: a visually exciting home for the university's approved top-ten rankings. Ranked students appear as comic-style illustrations, never personal photographs. Everyone can view the standings without signing in.

              01

              Find your level

              Choose Level 4, 5, or 6 to see that level's approved top ten.

              02

              Meet the characters

              Board artwork is assigned by rank from the illustrated cast. Default illustrated characters are shown until students choose their own; they are not likenesses of the students. No personal images and no image uploads.

              03

              See who tops the board

              Show approved names and ranked positions, never individual grades, scores, or attendance records.

              Rankings are published by authorized university staff from approved quiz results and show only names and positions. Characters on the board are illustrations assigned by rank, not photos or likenesses of students.

              YOUR ARENA ACCOUNT
