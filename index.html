    BIG ENERGY.
    SMALL SCOPE.

    Academic Arena starts with one thing: a visually exciting home for the university's approved top-ten rankings. Students get comic-style characters instead of personal photographs. Everyone can view the standings; only authorized students will be able to update their assigned characters when the production account system is built.

    01

    Find your competition

    Choose a level, semester, and module. Groups match university-approved cohorts and module enrollments.

    02

    Own your character

    Personalize an illustrated look. No personal images and no free-form image uploads.

    03

    See who tops the board

    Show approved names and ranked positions, never individual grades, scores, or attendance records.

    V6 is an offline visual prototype containing fictional names and results. The avatar studio is a local demonstration, not a real authentication feature. Public rankings still require a university-approved disclosure policy.
