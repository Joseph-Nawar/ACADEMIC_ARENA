                  HOW IT WORKS

                  TOP TEN.
                  EVERY LEVEL.

                  Academic Arena celebrates the top quiz performers in Levels 4, 5 and 6 of UOL Computer Science at EUE. Anyone can view the standings, no sign-in needed.

                  01

                  Find your level

                  Switch between Level 4, 5 and 6 to see each level’s top ten.

                  02

                  How the ranking works

                  Students are ranked by quiz participation first, then quiz scores. Only names and positions are published.

                  03

                  The characters

                  Every ranked student appears as an illustration, never a photo or a likeness. Unless a student has chosen their own, it’s an anonymous competitor.

                  SIGNING IN

                  University staff give each student an Arena username and a temporary password. After your first sign-in you set your own password, then open My Character to pick your look. It appears on the leaderboard only once staff confirm your ranking and you switch it on.

                  YOUR ARENA ACCOUNT

                  Use the username and password issued by university staff. There’s no public sign-up, and you don’t need to sign in to view the leaderboards. Lost access? Contact the Arena staff team.

                  STAFF SECURITY CHECK

                  Add this one-time setup key to your authenticator app. Keep it private; it is shown only during setup.

                  NEXT: YOUR CHARACTER

                  Pick your look in My Character. Showing it on a leaderboard needs a staff-verified ranking link and your opt-in.
