                    HOW IT WORKS

                    SHOW UP.
                    SCORE UP.

                    Academic Arena celebrates the students who keep turning up. Every quiz you attend moves you forward, and every warning sets you back. Consistency is what gets you on the board.

                    YOUR ACADEMIC SCORE

                    • 0

                      Everyone starts at zero

                      Same starting line for every student.

                    • +1

                      Each quiz you attend

                      Show up and take part. Every quiz counts.

                    • −1

                      Each warning received

                      Attendance warnings take a point away.

                    • ±0

                      Normal attendance

                      It’s the expected minimum, so it earns no extra points. It keeps warnings away.

                    EXAMPLE

                    5 quizzes attended +5+2 warnings −2=ACADEMIC SCORE 3

                    HOW SCORES ARE RANKED

                    Highest Academic Score first. If two students are level, the one with the higher total of quiz scores goes ahead. Week 5 is a special case; see the note below.

                    01

                    Never skip a quiz

                    Each one you attend adds a point to your score.

                    02

                    Stay warning-free

                    Steady attendance keeps your points safe.

                    03

                    Keep it consistent

                    Week after week, showing up is what climbs the board.

                    Private by design. Only names and positions are published. Academic Scores, quiz marks, attendance and warnings are never shown.

                    About the Week 5 boards: warnings weren’t part of the Week 5 data, so those boards rank quizzes attended, then quiz scores. For a student with no warnings, that’s the same as the Academic Score.

                    01

                    Find your level

                    Switch between Level 4, 5 and 6 to see each level’s top ten. Anyone can view the standings; no sign-in needed.

                    02

                    The characters

                    Every ranked student appears as an illustrated 3D character, never a photo or a likeness. Staff choose a default character for each published ranking, and students with an Arena account can swap in their own.

                    SIGNING IN

                    University staff give each student an Arena username and a temporary password. After your first sign-in you set your own password, then sign in again with it and open My Character to pick your look. It appears on the leaderboard only once staff confirm your ranking and you switch it on.

                    YOUR ARENA ACCOUNT

                    Use the username and password issued by university staff. There’s no public sign-up, and you don’t need to sign in to view the leaderboards. Lost access? Contact the Arena staff team.
